# Rotonda Privacy Policy

**Last updated: November 20, 2026**

Rotonda is an app for studying Italian driving-theory tests. It is developed by Zhi. This policy explains how the app handles information, including when you use a TestFlight beta.

## Study information stays on your device

Rotonda does not require an account. The app stores your study answers, progress, exam history, review cards, saved questions and words, reading positions, preferences, and cached word translations on your device. Progress is maintained separately for each selected licence.

The app and its Home Screen widgets share the local information needed to show your study status and handle widget interactions. Rotonda does not send this study information to a developer-operated server.

Your device's backup features may include local app data, depending on your system settings. Those backups are managed through your device and backup provider.

## Analytics, advertising, and tracking

The current app does not include third-party advertising or analytics SDKs, and it does not track you across other companies' apps or websites. We do not sell your personal information or use it for targeted advertising.

Apple's TestFlight services and websites you choose to open have separate data practices, described below.

## Translation and speech

Question banks and available bundled translations can be used offline. For vocabulary that has no bundled meaning in your selected language, Rotonda can use Apple's on-device translation features when the required language packs are installed. Generated word meanings are cached on your device.

Language-pack downloads and other Apple system services are subject to Apple's privacy practices. Reading text aloud uses Apple's speech-synthesis features. Rotonda does not record your voice.

## Optional online dictionaries

You can choose to open external dictionaries, including WordReference and Wiktionary, in an in-app Safari browser. The word you look up is included in the website request. The website receives connection information such as your IP address and may use cookies, analytics, or advertising under its own policies.

These lookups require an internet connection. They are optional, and the websites' practices are separate from Rotonda's local study features.

## Sharing study images

If you choose to share a study image, the image and any study statistics shown on it are passed to the app or service you select in the system share sheet. Review the image before sharing it. The recipient app or service handles the shared information under its own privacy policy.

## TestFlight beta testing

When you install a beta through TestFlight, Apple provides the developer with testing information such as installation and session statistics, device and operating-system information, and crash reports. Feedback you choose to send may include your comments, screenshots, and contact information. Information available to the developer can differ depending on how you joined the beta and what you submit.

We use this information to investigate problems, respond to feedback, and improve Rotonda. Avoid including information you do not want to share in feedback or screenshots. Apple's TestFlight terms and privacy information explain how Apple handles beta-testing data.

## Contact and support

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you include. We use it to answer your request and resolve the issue. Please send only the information needed to explain the problem.

We retain support correspondence and feedback for as long as reasonably needed to respond, investigate issues, and maintain the app, or to meet applicable legal obligations. When processing this information is subject to data-protection law, our purposes include responding to your request, our legitimate interest in maintaining and improving the app, and compliance with applicable legal obligations.

## Your choices and rights

You can use **Reset all progress** in the app's settings to remove study answers, review cards, and exam history from the device. This is a study-progress reset, not a request to delete correspondence or feedback held outside the app. Removing the app removes its local app data; device backups and data you have shared elsewhere may remain under their respective settings and policies.

You can stop beta testing through TestFlight and manage installed translation languages and device backups through Apple's system settings.

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to processing of personal information, and to request portability or complain to your local data-protection authority. Contact us about information you have sent to us. We cannot retrieve study history that remains solely on your device.

Apple and external services may process information in countries other than where you live. Their privacy notices describe their processing and applicable safeguards.

## Changes to this policy

We will update this policy when Rotonda's data practices change and revise the date above. Material changes will be communicated as appropriate.
