# Explanation reference sources

These are starting points checked on 17 November 2026, not a substitute for reading
the provisions that support each explanation. Record the article, paragraph, edition,
working URL and supporting point in draft evidence. Reviewers check them independently.
Do not cite a search snippet or a URL that you could not inspect.

For road rules, ACI publishes individual articles:

- [Article 141: speed](https://aci.gov.it/codice-della-strada/art-141/)
- [Article 148: overtaking](https://aci.gov.it/codice-della-strada/art-148/)
- [Article 158: stopping and parking prohibitions](https://aci.gov.it/codice-della-strada/art-158/)

The individual pages were retrievable even though `/codice-della-strada/` returned
404. Discover and open each other required article; do not assume an invented URL
works. Read qualifications and exceptions, not just the first sentence of a paragraph.

For sign definitions and figures, the Comune di Napoli publishes a
[213-page copy of DPR 495/1992](https://static-www.comune.napoli.it/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/DPR___19921216.495___Regolamento_di_esecuzione_e_attuazione_c.d.s.__aggiornato_d.P.R._2012.151_.pdf).
Its cover states that it is updated through DPR 151/2012. It is an explicitly dated
reference, not proof of current law: check subsequent changes and applicability to
the question's source edition. Match the actual attached quiz figure as well as the
legal description. For other technical or specialist subjects, find the applicable
authoritative source rather than stretching these references beyond their scope.

For driving/rest periods and tachographs, start with the applicable consolidated
versions of [Regulation 561/2006](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/PDF/?uri=CELEX%3A02006R0561-20241231)
and [Regulation 165/2014](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/PDF/?uri=CELEX%3A02014R0165-20241231).
Those links identify the 31 December 2024 consolidations, not a guarantee that no
later amendment applies. EUR-Lex sometimes returns a JavaScript challenge even at
a PDF URL; that response is not the regulation. Obtain inspectable primary text.
The [TRACE II tachograph guide](https://transport.ec.europa.eu/system/files/2023-12/TRACE_II_Tachograph_Enforcement_Guidance.pdf)
contains useful comparisons, but its own disclaimer identifies it as nonbinding
contractor guidance. Distinguish its historical quotations from applicable law.
For device behavior, check the manufacturer's model and variant: an ADR-specific
ignition procedure does not establish how every tachograph reads a card.

For Italian rear identification panels, use the complete **DM 24 January 2003,
no. 40**, including the installation diagrams in Annex A, printed page 8 of
[GU 61, Ordinary Supplement 42](https://www.gazzettaufficiale.it/eli/gu/2003/03/14/61/so/42/sg/pdf).
The ordinary issue without that supplement is a different PDF. Read installation
layouts and vehicle scope separately from product type-approval dimensions;
counting drawn segments alone can misidentify the number of panel elements.

Reflective contour strips use a different document: **DM 27 December 2004**, in
[GU 44 of 23 February 2005](https://www.gazzettaufficiale.it/eli/gu/2005/02/23/44/sg/pdf).
Annex A section 5, printed page 23, supplies the color provisions. An article-only
page omitting the annex cannot support those details. Orange dangerous-goods
plates are another category; check the applicable ADR section 5.3.2 rather than
transferring requirements from rear identification panels or contour strips.

For international insurance documents, [UCI's green-card guidance](https://ucimi.it/carta-verde/)
distinguishes destinations requiring the document from those admitting Italian-registered
vehicles without it. A document exemption does not remove the insurance requirement.
Do not turn a destination list into a permanent rule for every non-EU country.

For CEMT freight permits, the **ITF 2026 User Guide** is also available as a
[Slovak transport-ministry copy](https://www.mindop.sk/fileadmin/documents/doprava/cesta/scd/CEMT_info_v%C5%A1eobecn%C3%A9/user_guide_2026_CEMT.pdf).
Its introduction and sections 4–6 describe the January 2026 digital transition.
Distinguish the digital licence and electronic logbook, their generated information
documents available digitally or printed, and separately required vehicle certificates.
A printed current information document is not a photocopy of an older paper licence.
Qualify older quiz wording visibly when it describes the superseded paper process.

For accident-report forms, inspect **IVASS Regulation 56/2025, Annex 1**, including
the [form and its instructions](https://www.ivass.it/normativa/nazionale/secondaria-ivass/regolamenti/2025/n56/Regolamento_56_25.03.2025.pdf).
Instruction 3 permits additional forms for more than two vehicles. Form usage is
distinct from eligibility for direct compensation. Distinguish owner, policyholder
and driver; a field's presence alone does not establish a universal legal duty.
For accident-scene duties, read [road-code article 189](https://aci.gov.it/codice-della-strada/art-189/)
and, for roadway obstructions, [article 161](https://aci.gov.it/codice-della-strada/art-161/).
Preserving traces is subject to safety, and property-damage-only incidents require
avoiding obstruction where possible. An agency's internal fleet policy cannot prove
that every driver has the same reporting obligations.

For historical Italian bus equipment and exit counts, the complete **DM 18 April
1977** appears on printed pages 3–9 of the [ordinary supplement to GU 135 of
19 May 1977](https://www.gazzettaufficiale.it/eli/gu/1977/05/19/135/so/0/sg/pdf).
This is a scanned PDF: inspect the page images, not empty extracted text.
Section 5.5.8.1 on page 6 establishes the historical extinguisher quantities and
units, including the boundary at 30 seats and approved equivalents. Section 5.5.8.2
on page 7 specifies first-aid **storage space**, not the actual kit count or contents.
Section 5.6.2.1.1 on page 7 concerns **emergency exits** by passenger count excluding
the driver; section 2.7 permits qualifying service doors to count. Do not substitute
the related UN R36 total-exit table for the Italian annex or silently equate seated
places with total passenger capacity. Check later requirements and the vehicle's
approval before presenting a historical provision as a universal current rule.

For tyre markings, the [official 2008 publication of UN R54](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/PDF/?uri=CELEX:42008X0711(01))
contains the commercial-tyre service-description examples and load-index table.
It incorporates supplement 16; identify that edition rather than claiming to have
inspected an inaccessible later UNECE PDF. For Italian tread requirements, read
**DPR 495/1992, Appendix VIII, article 237(1)(a)**: distinguish visible tread over
the width and circumference from minimum depth in the principal grooves. Preserve
the vehicle categories when localizing the numerical limits.

The [USTMA 2017 commercial truck and bus tyre manual](https://www.ustires.org/sites/default/files/2024-08/Care%20and%20Service%20of%20Commercial%20Truck%20and%20Bus%20Tires%202017%20.pdf)
distinguishes conditional structural sidewall repairs (printed page 43), damage
outside the repairable areas, and matching dual tyres (page 51). This is US technical
guidance, not Italian legal authorization. Do not transfer passenger-car repair
restrictions into a universal claim about every commercial tyre.
Continental's [commercial-tyre white paper](https://cdn.continental.com/fileadmin/__imported/sites/corporate/_international/english/hubpages/10_20press/01_press_releases/06_20cvt/001_white_papers_cvt/white_paper_tires_for_commercial_vehicles.pdf)
explains load support, traction and lateral guidance on pages 7–8. Its
[2025 product data guide](https://www.continental-tires.com/content/dam/conti-tires-cms/continental/market-content/us/truck/literature/catalogs/TT-CO-BK-DATA.pdf)
provides size, inflation and single/dual load tables on printed page 96 (PDF page 97).
These tables do not establish unrelated legal exceptions or operating rules.

For wheel servicing, the [UD Quon November 2023 owner's manual](https://www.udtrucks.com/sites/default/files/2024-07/Quon%20Owners%20Manual%20-%20Nov%2023.pdf)
explains its wheel-nut procedure on printed pages 13-43–51. Preserve its vehicle and
wheel-system scope; another model may use a different sequence or specified torque.
For clutch slip, [ZF's workshop guidance](https://aftermarket.zf.com/en/aftermarket-portal/knowledge-hub/clutch-slipping/)
separates worn facings, contamination and loss of clamping force. A generic parts
catalogue or materials page is not evidence for a mechanism it does not describe.

For integrated pneumatic protection units, the [WABCO C-APU testing instructions,
edition 1, June 2016](https://www.wabco-customercentre.com/catalog/docs/9325019513.pdf)
separate the multi-circuit protection unit's pressure-limitation test (section 4.4.1,
printed page 12) from the dryer/governor cutout test (section 4.4.2, page 13).
The former tests regulated trailer and auxiliary outlets, not a failure of the main
service-reservoir gauge. A mechanism inferred from these functions must retain that
configuration scope; the test procedure is not a documented fault incident or proof
of an ambiguous quiz's intended meaning. Do not include workshop adjustment steps
in learner explanations.

For warning symbols, inspect the exact edition of UN R121 and the correct row of
Table 1. A control symbol, a tell-tale and a mandatory colour are distinct entries;
an adjacent row's colour does not establish the requirement for the question's
symbol. Record the actual source figure separately from a regulation's illustration.

Large manufacturer PDFs may exceed the browser's extraction limit. A complete
local reference copy outside the repository can be inspected directly; record the
original URL, edition, hash and actual printed/PDF page. Keep these reference
downloads separate from manually supplied official quiz inputs. If only an indexed
copy was inspected, record that access precisely and inspect the supporting section,
not just a search snippet. If a cited site fails, locate another authoritative copy
and verify its content before replacing the citation. Never mark a blocked source
as inspected.
