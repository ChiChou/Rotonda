# Spanish terminology review

The full batch review was followed by a cross-range Terra review of 83 Italian/Spanish pairs selected for sensitive terminology. The original batch reviewers confirmed follow-up changes and resealed the affected files. Per-batch JSON reports retain the final corrections and reasons.

- Preserve false assertions: review concerns translation fidelity, not whether an Italian quiz claim is true.
- Distinguish ordinary parking (`sosta`) from temporary stopping (`fermata`), while translating emergency stopping by context rather than mechanically calling it parking.
- Translate `piazzola di sosta` as a roadside lay-by (`apartadero`) rather than implying a designated parking lot or bus stop.
- Preserve the inclusion of Saturdays in Italian `giorni feriali`; avoid an unqualified “días hábiles” that may exclude Saturdays.
- Preserve the complete vehicle class in `filoveicoli`. [Article 55 published by ACI](https://aci.gov.it/codice-della-strada/art-55/) defines electrically powered, overhead-fed vehicles that are not rail-guided. The Spanish wording “vehículos eléctricos sin rieles alimentados por línea aérea” retains that scope without narrowing it to passenger trolleybuses or broadening it to trams. This terminology check does not change any quiz assertion about the documents required to drive them.
- Keep machine prefixes such as `13##tipologia13` unchanged; translate their human-readable label or date.

These are model reviews against the Italian sources, with file and source fingerprints. They do not constitute human certification.
