VOCABULARY DATA — SOURCES AND LIMITATIONS

The quiz bank is unchanged. word_study_metadata.json is a separately generated vocabulary overlay. The CEFR levels are estimates from an automatically/LLM-assisted public learning deck, not official Council of Europe assessments. Surface forms are matched exactly first. Morphological matches inherit a level only if every possible lemma has a source level and those levels agree. Missing/conflicting matches remain unclassified. Only source entries used by the overlay are included. Source SHA-256 values are recorded in the JSON. The generator is Scripts/build_word_study_metadata.py.
