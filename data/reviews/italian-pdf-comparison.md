Extracted 7,106 distinct question IDs and VERO/FALSO answers from the PDF, matched by ID to each database, and compared the Italian text after Unicode NFKC and whitespace normalization. All 7,106 answers and texts match in `Rotonda/Data/quiz.db`. All 7,106 answers match in `PatenteData/database/quiz.db`, but its 32 texts below omit a short word. The affected PDF rows were visually checked in rendered page crops. These are differences between the two databases, not defects in the app input.

Every database question ID, including 31011–31064, was extracted from the PDF, and no extracted PDF ID is missing from either database. This comparison does not check image assets or translations.

## Method and limitations

Text and answer values were extracted page by page with `pypdf`, locating rows by five-digit question number and the VERO/FALSO marker. Each extracted number was unique; every extracted ID matched a database row. Line breaks and other whitespace were collapsed, and Unicode NFKC normalization applied before comparison. Answers map VERO to 1 and FALSO to 0. The PDF table text is machine-readable; the 32 discrepant rows were also checked visually. This is a text and answer comparison to this specific PDF, not an independent validation of current legal rules.
